Readiness

    How applicants and approvers use it

    1. The applicant sends PERMIT to the bot's number and gets a template to fill in.
    2. The bot posts the request to the admin group.
    3. An approver replies to it with APPROVE (optional remark) or REJECT reason, or types APPROVE 0042.
    4. The applicant receives the PDF letter, the reason, or an expiry notice. Their details are then deleted.

    Admin group

    The bot must be a member of the group. Requests wait until a group is set.

    Timeout

    1–48 hours. Applies to new requests only.

    Spam guard

    Stops one number flooding the admin group. A number sending more than 10 PERMIT messages a minute is ignored. Counts reset if the bot restarts.
